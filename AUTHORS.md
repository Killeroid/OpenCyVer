# Authors

OpenCyVer is based on CyVer, which was created by:

* Ioanna Mandilara
* Christina-Maria Androna

The original project, and its releases up to 2.0.2 (Neo4j), live at
<https://gitlab.com/netmode/CyVer>. The Cypher validation approach, the patterns and
the three validators (`SyntaxValidator`, `SchemaValidator`, `PropertiesValidator`) come
from that work. If you use OpenCyVer in research, please cite the paper listed in the README.

The FalkorDB version (3.0.0 and later) was ported and is maintained by Kwaku Yeboah-Antwi.
This repository's history starts at that version.
